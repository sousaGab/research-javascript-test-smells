\subsection{Validation Metric Definitions}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Comprehensive Validation Metrics}
\label{tab:metrics-definitions}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.5cm}p{5cm}p{4.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Definition} & \textbf{Computation} \\ \midrule
\textbf{Smell Removal Rate} & 
  Percentage of experiments where target smell eliminated & 
  $\frac{\text{smell\_removed} = \text{True}}{\text{total}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Test Pass Rate} & 
  Percentage where tests still pass after refactoring & 
  $\frac{\text{tests\_passing} = \text{True}}{\text{total}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Coverage Preservation} & 
  Percentage where coverage maintained or improved & 
  $\frac{\text{coverage\_decreased} = \text{False}}{\text{total}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{New Smell Rate} & 
  Percentage introducing new smells & 
  $\frac{\text{new\_smells} = \text{True}}{\text{total}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Success Rate} & 
  Combined: smell removed AND tests pass AND no new smells & 
  $\frac{\text{all conditions met}}{\text{total}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Cost Efficiency} & 
  Tokens per successful refactoring & 
  $\frac{\sum \text{tokens}}{\text{successes}}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Mean Latency} & 
  Average LLM response time & 
  $\frac{\sum \text{latency}}{n}$ \\
\addlinespace
\textbf{Semantic Preservation} & 
  Test assertions unchanged (manual review) & 
  Qualitative assessment \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Statistical Analysis Methods}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Statistical Tests Applied}
\label{tab:statistical-tests}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3cm}p{4.5cm}p{5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Test} & \textbf{Purpose} & \textbf{Application} \\ \midrule
Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) & 
  Independence of categorical variables & 
  Compare smell removal rates across strategies \\
\addlinespace
ANOVA & 
  Difference in means across groups & 
  Compare token usage, latency across models \\
\addlinespace
Tukey HSD & 
  Post-hoc pairwise comparisons & 
  Identify which model/strategy pairs differ \\
\addlinespace
Mann-Whitney U & 
  Non-parametric group comparison & 
  Compare success rates (non-normal distribution) \\
\addlinespace
Kruskal-Wallis & 
  Non-parametric alternative to ANOVA & 
  Compare latency across models (skewed data) \\
\addlinespace
Fisher's Exact & 
  Small sample categorical analysis & 
  Compare success in low-frequency smells \\
\addlinespace
Effect Size (Cohen's d) & 
  Practical significance & 
  Quantify magnitude of strategy differences \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
